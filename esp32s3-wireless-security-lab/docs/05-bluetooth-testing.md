# 05 — Bluetooth / BLE Exploration

## Objective

Explore Bluetooth Low Energy discovery and advertising behaviour.

## Lab method

Use your own BLE devices or devices for which you have permission.

Observe:

- Advertised device names
- BLE advertisements
- Signal-strength changes
- Device appearance/disappearance during movement

## Security lesson

BLE advertisements can reveal information about nearby devices. IoT developers should minimize unnecessary information exposure and protect sensitive operations with appropriate authentication and encryption.
